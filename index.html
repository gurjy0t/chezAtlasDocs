How to make your coffee — Chez Atlas Guest Suite
1
Turn the black knob on top to the right, toward the open-lock icon, to unlock the head.
2
Grab a pod from the container next to the machine and drop it in, foil side up.
3
Turn the knob to the left, toward the closed-lock icon, until it stops — this pierces the capsule and locks the head shut.
4
Place your cup on the drip tray under the spout.
5
Press the cup-icon button in the center of the knob. The machine reads a barcode printed on the pod and automatically brews the correct size and strength — no buttons to select, no guessing.
6
Coffee starts pouring within a few seconds. When it stops, your cup is ready — turn the knob to the right (unlock) once more and the used pod ejects automatically into the internal bin.
💡 Tip: The water tank is already filled. If the machine won't start, check that the tank is pushed fully into place at the back — it needs a good seat to make contact.
Good to know
  • Every pod brews a different size automatically — no need to hold the button or press it more than once.
  • If the light on the button is blinking, the machine is heating up (~15–20 sec). Wait for it to turn solid, then press.
  • The machine auto-shuts off after about 9 minutes of inactivity — just tap the button again to wake it up.
  • Used pods collect inside the machine. No need to empty it during your stay.
  • Pods are for one use only — please don't reuse or refill them.
Enjoy your stay — Chez Atlas Guest Suite
