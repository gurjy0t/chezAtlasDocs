How to use the TV — Chez Atlas Guest Suite
1
Turn on the TV by pressing the red power button at the top of the remote.
2
Adjust the volume using the two long buttons on the right edge of the remote.
3
Press the house-shaped Home button any time to get back to the main menu.
4
For free live TV, from the Home screen press Left on the purple direction pad and select the Live TV tile. It's free, ad-supported live channels — no login or subscription needed.
5
Netflix, Disney+, Apple TV+, and Crave each have their own button on the remote — but we don't provide subscriptions to these. If you'd like to use your own account, press the app's button and sign in.
6
To connect your own device (laptop, game console, streaming stick), plug it into an open HDMI port on the back of the TV. Then from the Home screen, scroll over to find the matching HDMI tile and select it with OK.
🔒 Before you check out: if you signed into any of your own streaming accounts, please sign back out so the next guest doesn't see your account.
Troubleshooting
  • Remote not responding? Check the batteries, and make sure nothing is blocking the sensor on the TV.
  • TV won't turn on? Check that the power cable is firmly plugged in at both the wall and the back of the TV.
  • No picture after plugging in HDMI? Make sure you selected the matching HDMI tile on the Home screen for the port you used.
  • Can't find the HDMI tile? Go to the TV's Settings → TV Inputs and select it from there instead.
